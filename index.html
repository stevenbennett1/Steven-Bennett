From builder to backer.

04. Philosophy

Built to dominate, not just to compete.

Steven doesn't invest in incremental improvement. He backs the founders building the layer of technology that decides who controls the next century of infrastructure, compute, and industry. Deep tech isn't a niche, it's the substrate the entire global economy gets rebuilt on, and he backs the founders who intend to own it.

The next trillion dollars of value won't go to the fastest follower. It will go to whoever controls the hardest layer.

That conviction wasn't inherited, it was built one company at a time. It started with the first product he shipped in 2015 and ran through years of fundraising, hiring, and failing in public before getting it right. Every investment since has been made with that same operator's discipline: get in early, work alongside the founder, and stay in the room long after the term sheet is signed. That's how a handful of early bets compounds into a portfolio built for global scale.

Own the hard layer

Back technology that's difficult to copy and impossible to route around.

Operate, don't spectate

Work alongside founders as a partner who has actually built, not just funded.

Compound relentlessly

Every company, every cycle, sharpens the thesis and the network for the next one.
